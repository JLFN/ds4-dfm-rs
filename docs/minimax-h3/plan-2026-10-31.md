# Plan: run the Sol-H3 pipeline as the page does

Goal: the published shape on one Spark, 1344x768 at 24 fps for 5 s, with the
content the draft drew and the speed the page reports, from this branch.

Baselines below are measured on gx10-e1d2, not quoted from the page, except
where the page is named. Every phase has an acceptance test and a rollback;
none is adopted on a single run.

## Where we are

| route | wall | sharpness | content |
|---|---|---|---|
| fast faithful (4-step draft, upscale, H3 decode) | 150.2 s | 14.11 | draft's, but that draft buries the head at 0.5 s |
| fast faithful, FP8 stage 1 | 108.5 s | 20.64 | one fox, head and single tail at 0.5-3.5 s |
| fast faithful, 8-step draft | ~173 s (denoise 44.45 s) | not yet measured | correct at 0.5, 1.0 and 2.0 s |
| direct canvas, 20 steps | 630.3 s | 17.54 | correct at 0.5 to 5.0 s |
| published recipe as we can run it | 110.4 s refine + 15.3 s decode | 5.54 | replaced |

Published bar: 56.17 s, of which 19.77 s H3 generation, 24.71 s refinement,
7.02 s LTX decode, 2.13 s Qwen, and a resident footprint of 116.9 GiB out of
119.68.

## Phase 0 - turn on the FP8 stage 1 we already have

The port carries the release's W8A8 route behind `H3_DIT_GEMM=fp8`
(`crates/ds4-core/src/minimax_h3/dit.rs:1526-1563`), with per-matrix scales
taken from `fastvideo/layers/quantization/fp8_config.py`, and it is already
measured (`docs/minimax-h3/fp8-route-2026-10-29.md:90-124`): the four
projections fall from 8,225 ms to 3,803 per evaluation and the render-level
figures are denoise 54.93 s to 31.89 s and load 32.81 s to 21.69 s at that
record's shape.

Nobody has run it at the shapes this plan uses. Do that first: it is an
environment variable, no code.

Acceptance: at 672x384x124 and at 1344x768, denoise and load fall without the
anatomy changing at 0.5, 1.0 and 2.0 s, and the sharpness number stays inside
the noise floor of the BF16 arm at the same seed. Rollback: unset
`H3_DIT_GEMM`.

Result at 672x384x124, one run: 150.202 s to 108.526 s, denoise 21.810 to
14.971, load 39.167 to 8.226, sharpness 14.11 to 20.64, and the arms sample
different single foxes (`docs/minimax-h3/sol-attn-paper-2026-10-31.md`
section 1). The 1344x768 arm is still unmeasured.

## Phase 1 - the draft is the quality floor

Measured: the 4-step draft buries the fox's head at 0.5 s and grows a second
tail at 1.0 s; the upscale reproduces both exactly because it is the same
latent; 8 steps at the same prompt and seed is correct at all three sampled
times, and the 20-step direct render is correct across the clip.

Two levers, both cheap, and they are not exclusive:

1. steps: 8 measured at 44.45 s of denoise, against 21.40 at 4 steps;
2. prompt: the page's showcase prompts are 200 to 600 words with subject
   definitions, shot timing and explicit "preserve anatomy" clauses; ours is
   15 tokens. Run the same 4-step arm with such a prompt and see whether the
   anatomy holds.

Acceptance: no missing head, no duplicated limb or tail, no extra animal, at
0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 3.5 and 4.5 s, in the draft and after the upscale. Adopt the
cheapest setting that passes.

## Phase 2 - residency

`crates/ds4-server/src/h3_engine.rs` already owns a resident H3 engine. A
process per request pays 39.2 s of DiT load and 15.4 s of cold Qwen; the
residential route pays neither. This is the largest single step available and
it needs no new kernels.

Acceptance: two consecutive requests in one process, the second showing load
and prompt-encode at or near zero, with both outputs identical to the
one-shot route at the same seed.

## Phase 3 - the decode is the faithful route's bottleneck

The H3 video VAE decode at 1344x768 costs 65.2 s of the 150.2 s route, while
the LTX decode the page uses costs 7.0 s. The release decodes in four tiles
and streams the output; our port decodes the whole clip in one pass
(`docs/gb10-hardware.md`, tiling note). This, not the refiner, is what stands
between the faithful route and the published number.

Acceptance: same frame sharpness and a decoded frame identical to the untiled
route at a fixed seed, with the phase under 30 s. Rollback: keep the untiled
call behind a flag.

## Phase 4 - only then, the refiner's speed

If the published recipe is still wanted, profile one refinement step with
nsys before changing anything. 110.4 s for three steps is 36.8 s each, and
the page's 24.71 s for the same three cannot be reconciled with 66 to 126 GB
of weights through a 273 GB/s memory system, so the profile decides whether
the difference is a kernel inefficiency we can close or a configuration we
do not have. No FP8 refiner work before that profile exists.

## Phase 5 - recipe parity, not content

Port the release's frozen audio state and the joint audio-video call
(`runtime/stage2.py:78-99`; our `ltx_stage2.rs` passes `audio=None`). This
closes the last functional difference from the published route. It will not
stop the refiner from replacing the subject; the review in
`docs/minimax-h3/reference-latency-2026-10-31.md` section 9a records why.

## What the target costs

With Phase 0 done the faithful route measures 108.5 s; with Phase 2 also
resident it becomes roughly 0 text + 0 load + 15 s denoise + 3 s audio +
2.8 s upscale + 65 s decode + 0.9 s mux, about 87 s. Phase 3 at 30 s of
decode brings it to about 52 s, and at 20 s to about 42 s. So the published
56 s is reachable on the *faithful* route without ever running the LTX
refiner, and the decode is the number to attack.

## Rules for every phase

- One GPU job at a time, under `tools/host_memory_guard.py`.
- Record the phase line, the output hash and the sharpness/fidelity pair.
- A change lands with its measurement, and the fallback stays reachable.
- Content is checked across the clip, not at one frame.
